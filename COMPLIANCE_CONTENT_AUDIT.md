# Compliance content audit

## Findings addressed

- Old source material used “financially free” language. The rebuilt site uses education, clarity and independent judgement instead and expressly avoids promised financial outcomes.
- Old NDA wording “effective always” has been replaced by a review draft requiring identified parties, purpose, duration and signature.
- Old privacy wording implied unspecified plugins could access data. The new inventory identifies only detected front-end forms, local consent storage, XAUS and GDELT, and flags unknown infrastructure.
- Old terms used unconditional refund language. The new draft preserves statutory rights and flags cooling-off/refund wording for solicitor approval.
- No verified FCA, FOS, FSCS, broker-partnership, win-rate or guaranteed-profit claim was detected in the rebuilt React content.
- Bot screens and data are labelled concepts; regulatory/legal review is required before execution is enabled.

## Items requiring business approval

- “5+ years FX trading” and “10+ years leadership” are founder/business claims already supplied by AOS; retain supporting evidence.
- Affiliate rate “up to 20%” requires a separate approved agreement and tracking/payment rules.
- Prices and product inclusions are commercial claims sourced from `src/data/siteContent.ts`; review whenever plans change.
