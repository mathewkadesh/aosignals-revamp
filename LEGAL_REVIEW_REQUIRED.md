# Legal and compliance review required

Final legal wording, regulatory classification, FCA perimeter position, cancellation policy, refund policy, bot functionality and platform relationships must be reviewed and approved by a qualified UK solicitor or financial-services compliance professional before publication.

## Launch questions

1. Is AOS FCA authorised, does it have an FRN, or is it an appointed representative?
2. Has an authorised firm approved any financial promotions?
3. Which instruments are discussed: forex, CFDs, cryptoassets, securities or others?
4. Are signals real-time, instrument-specific or personalised?
5. Does the bot execute or copy trades, receive credentials, hold money or arrange platform accounts?
6. Are broker referral payments received and which platforms are integrated?
7. Which countries are served and which are prohibited?
8. What are the exact cancellation, refund, trial and digital-access rules?
9. How can customers cancel and when does access end?
10. Which payment processor is used and is VAT applicable?
11. Which cookies and personal-data categories are used in production?
12. Where is data hosted, which processors receive it and does it leave the UK?
13. What are the approved retention periods?
14. Is AOS registered with the ICO and what is the number?
15. What are the legal, privacy, complaints, support and fraud-reporting contacts?
16. Is professional indemnity or cyber insurance held?
17. Who owns the bot, code, brand and educational content?
18. Which NDA mode and confidentiality period are required?
19. Is the service B2C, B2B or both?
20. Does the affiliate programme have separate approved terms?
21. Has a UK financial-services solicitor approved launch?
